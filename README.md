# Sway Harner

I’m a software engineer with a background in frontend development, full-stack systems, automation, and design. Most of my work has centered around practical problems: understanding the system in front of me, finding the weak points, and building something that makes the experience simpler or more reliable for the people using it.

My background started in design, and that still shapes how I approach engineering. I care about how something works, but also how it feels to use, how maintainable it is, and whether the solution actually fits the problem.

## How I tend to work

I’m usually at my best when there’s something unfamiliar to figure out. I like working through problems patiently, understanding why something is failing before reaching for a fix, and keeping the solution as straightforward as the situation allows.

I’ve worked across a fairly wide range of technologies over the years, so I don’t treat a long list of tools as the same thing as current fluency. I’m actively refreshing and measuring the areas I use most now, especially JavaScript, React, TypeScript, Node.js, system design, and modern AI infrastructure.
